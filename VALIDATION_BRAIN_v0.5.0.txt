Storage policy defaults
-----------------------
- Offline Brain: auto-save when motion >= 220/1000.
- Online Brain: auto-save moving unknowns when motion >= 90 and familiarity < 760/1000.
- Online Brain: auto-save novel motion when motion >= 90 and novelty >= 560/1000.
- Automatic save cooldown: 5000 ms.
- Explicit teach/reward/manual anti-spam cooldown: 350 ms.
- Missing/failing SD does not stop vision telemetry.

Safety / authority invariants retained
--------------------------------------
- PS4/manual remains highest user-control authority.
- RA4M1 retains motor, servo, heartbeat, sonar-forward, robot-mode and movement-deadline safety gates.
- Fisheye remains perception/storage-only and has no motor authority.
- Camera SD writes never occur inside the RA4M1 real-time motor/safety loop.
- Imitation policy remains shadow-only by default.
- Existing BRAKE / radar 500 cm / no-echo forward-clear behavior remains unchanged.

Fisheye wiring change
---------------------
- SD_MMC 1-bit: GPIO14 CLK, GPIO15 CMD, GPIO2 D0.
- Fisheye UART: GPIO13 TX -> S3 GPIO41 RX.
- Fisheye UART: GPIO4 RX <- S3 GPIO42 TX.
- Old v0.4 GPIO14 UART TX wiring MUST NOT be retained because GPIO14 is now SD clock.
