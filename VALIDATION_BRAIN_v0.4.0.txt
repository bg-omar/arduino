Safety / authority invariants retained
--------------------------------------
- PS4/manual remains the highest user-control authority.
- RA4M1 retains motor, servo, heartbeat, sonar-forward, robot-mode, and movement-deadline safety gates.
- Fisheye remains perception-only and has no motor authority.
- Imitation execution is SHADOW ONLY by default.
- Learned actions may execute only when imitation policy is explicitly enabled, Brain is armed,
  manual control is inactive, robot modes are inactive, RA telemetry is fresh, and confidence >= 680/1000.
- Obstacle context forces BRAKE before imitation policy evaluation.
- PS4 demonstration capture is observation-only; existing PS4 drive/head control paths are not rerouted.
