Episode storage defaults
------------------------
Perception: 160x120 grayscale at normal camera loop rate
Episode sample rate: 5 fps
Default pre-event: 2000 ms -> up to 10 frames
Trigger frame: 1
Default post-event: 3000 ms -> about 15 frames
Typical raw payload: 26 * 19,200 B = 499,200 B plus small PGM/CSV metadata
PSRAM ring allocation: 25 * 19,200 B = 480,000 B (up to 5 s pre-history at 5 fps)
If PSRAM prebuffer allocation fails, post-event recording remains available but pre-history is absent.
